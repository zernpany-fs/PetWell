# PetWell — Decision Log: Consent-Based Access to Pet Records

| | |
|---|---|
| **Status** | Draft v1.2 |
| **Date** | 2026-11-27 (v1.2: D70–D71; v1.1: index of D55–D69, sections 6–7) |
| **Author** | Nopparuth Panyanithiwat |
| **Change** | Clinics can no longer read any pet's records by default. Access comes from a confirmed appointment or from the owner's explicit share. |

## 1. Why this change

The original design let any clinic search and read the full history of every pet ("clinic-agnostic passport"). That is convenient for clinics but gives owners no control over who sees their pet's health data. This change keeps the cross-clinic history (it is medically useful) and puts the owner in control of who can read it.

## 2. Decisions

| ID | Topic | Decision | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | When access starts (appointments) | When the clinic sets the appointment to **CONFIRMED** for the first time, from any status (not when the owner books). | A booking alone would let anyone book at random just to open a pet's data. |
| D2 | How long appointment-based access lasts | Until the appointment is **COMPLETED + 30 days**. | Covers follow-up questions after the visit without keeping access forever. |
| D3 | Owner share | The owner shares a pet with a clinic for **7 / 30 / 90 days or unlimited**, and can revoke at any time. | Owner chooses the trade-off between convenience and control. |
| D4 | Two kinds of access | (a) **Appointment-based** (automatic) and (b) **Owner share** (explicit). Cancelling or rejecting an appointment ends only (a). | An explicit owner share should not disappear because an appointment was cancelled. |
| D5 | What a clinic can read with access | The **full history from all clinics**. | A treating vet needs to know everything the pet has been through. |
| D6 | Records a clinic wrote itself | The authoring clinic **always** sees them, even without access. | They are the clinic's own work records. |
| D7 | Walk-in without an appointment | The owner generates a **share code** (OTP-style) and tells the clinic; the clinic enters it and gets access immediately. No request/approval step. Defaults: 6 digits, valid 15 minutes, single use, bound to one pet. Lockout after failed attempts: see D14. | Works at the counter in seconds; short expiry and single use limit abuse. |
| D8 | Pets with no owner in the system | A clinic can create the pet and its records itself. *Claiming* such a pet by an owner who registers later is out of scope. **Deferred to backlog for V2 — see D21.** | Needed for real walk-ins, but claim/merge logic is a separate, larger feature. |
| D9 | Owner contact info | While a clinic has access, it sees the contact info the owner entered. When access ends, it no longer sees it. | Practical for emergencies, and it follows the same access check as health records, so it needs no extra logic. What a clinic may keep after access ends is in the backlog. |
| D10 | Audit log | Record every time a clinic opens a pet (who, which pet, when, via which kind of access). | Trust and traceability. |
| D11 | After access ends (revoked or expired) | The clinic sees **only the records it authored**. It does not keep a frozen copy of other clinics' records. | Simple, and revocation really means revocation. Owners who want a clinic to keep access can use "unlimited" share. |
| D12 | Owner revokes a clinic's access | Revoking ends **both** appointment-based and owner-share access for that clinic. The appointment itself is not cancelled. | When an owner revokes a clinic, they mean that clinic should not see the pet at all. |
| D13 | Access after revocation | The clinic does **not** regain access from an appointment that was already confirmed. A later first-time `CONFIRMED` of another appointment (including one already pending at revoke time) creates new access. | Revocation must stick. An appointment the owner booked counts as fresh consent. |
| D14 | Share-code lockout | 5 wrong codes per staff account → that account is blocked for 15 min. 10 wrong codes per clinic per hour → code entry for the whole clinic is blocked for 15 min. | A per-account limit alone lets a clinic with many accounts keep guessing. |
| D15 | Ways to share | Owner-share access is granted **only via share code** (D7). | Keeps V2 within the timebox. Direct share to a chosen clinic goes to the backlog. |
| D16 | Where V2 lives | Same repo. Tag the last v1 commit as `v1.0`, build V2 on branch `feat/consent-access`, merge to `main` when done. Uncommitted v1 files must be cleared before tagging. | Reviewers can follow the system from v1 to v2 in one history. No copied code. |
| D17 | New entities | `PetAccessGrant` (both access types in one table: `type` = `APPOINTMENT` / `OWNER_SHARE`, `petId`, `clinicId`, `appointmentId?` (**unique**: one appointment creates at most one grant), `shareCodeId?` (**unique**, D25), `expiresAt?`, `endedAt?`, `endReason?` = `REVOKED` / `CANCELLED` / `REJECTED`, `createdAt`). `expiresAt = null` means no expiry date yet: unlimited for an owner share, not yet completed for an appointment grant. A grant is **active** when `endedAt` is null and `expiresAt` is null or in the future. `ShareCode` (`codeHash`, `petId`, `durationDays?`, `expiresAt`, `usedAt?`), `PetAccessLog` (audit log: `userId`, `clinicId`, `petId`, `grantId?` (D24), `viewedAt`), `ShareCodeAttempt` (lockout counting: `userId`, `clinicId`, `attemptedAt`, `success`). | One table for grants means one place for the access check, and D12 ends both types with one update. A unique `appointmentId` enforces US-01 AC3 and D13 in the database, not only in code. `endReason` shows why access ended; expiry needs no reason because it follows from `expiresAt`. Codes are stored as an HMAC (D26), so a database leak alone does not expose them. |
| D18 | Clinic pet search (`GET /pets/search`) | Results include only pets the clinic currently has access to, or pets with records the clinic authored. Owner contact (phone, email) is returned only for pets with active access. | Unrestricted search would bypass consent and expose owner contact (D9). |
| D19 | Writing health records | A clinic can create a record only while it has active access to the pet. Records it already authored stay editable after access ends (D6). | Stops clinics writing into histories of pets they do not treat. Walk-ins use a share code first. If the owner does not grant access, the visit is not recorded in PetWell; this gap is the owner's choice. |
| D20 | Response when a clinic has no access | No access and no authored records → `404 Pet not found` (does not reveal the pet exists). No access but has authored records → basic pet info (`id`, `name`, `species`) and only its own records. | Matches v1's 404 style and gives D6/D11 a concrete shape. |
| D21 | Scope cut: D8 | Pets without an owner (D8, US-05) move to the backlog for V2. | Making `Pet.ownerId` optional touches almost every ownership check and does not fit the 7-day timebox. |
| D22 | Test setup | Set up Vitest (minimal) on day 11 together with `.prettierrc`, and write access-check tests alongside the code. | Vitest is not installed yet; leaving setup to day 17 risks losing the test day. |
| D23 | Appointment completed after its grant ended | `COMPLETED` sets `expiresAt` = completion + 30 days **only on a grant that has not ended**. A grant that already ended (e.g. revoked) stays ended; the appointment still completes normally. | Revocation must stick (D13). D12 leaves the appointment untouched, so the clinic must still be able to complete it. |
| D24 | Audit log when a clinic has no grant | Log **every** time a clinic opens a pet. `PetAccessLog.grantId` is optional; null means the clinic viewed only its authored records (D6, D20). | D10 says every opening is recorded, including views without access. |
| D25 | Link a share code to its grant | `PetAccessGrant.shareCodeId?`, unique. | Shows which code created an owner share, and the database blocks a second grant from the same code (enforces single use, D7), even if two staff enter it at the same moment. |
| D26 | Share-code storage and generation | `codeHash` = HMAC-SHA256 with a secret from `.env` (`SHARE_CODE_SECRET`). Codes are generated with `crypto.randomInt`. On generate, if an unused, unexpired code has the same hash, generate again (max 5 tries). | A plain hash of a 6-digit code can be reversed by trying all 1,000,000 values; without the secret it cannot. The same code always gives the same hash, so redeeming is a direct lookup. Clinics enter only the code, so two live codes must never collide. |
| D27 | Repeat share and extension | Redeeming a new code for a clinic that already has an owner share creates a **new grant**; the clinic has access while any grant is active. The **owner** (not the clinic) can change the duration of an active `OWNER_SHARE` grant from the access list: 7 / 30 / 90 days or unlimited, counted from now. Ended grants and appointment-based grants cannot be extended. | Owners of a regular clinic should not have to generate a new code every time. Only the owner decides how long access lasts (D3), and an ended grant stays ended (D13). |
| D28 | Completed appointment with no grant | An appointment that reaches `COMPLETED` with no grant (v1 data confirmed before the V2 migration) gets **no grant change**. Legacy data is handled by reseeding; a backfill script is in the backlog. | Portfolio project with no real customer data. Keeps the grant logic identical to the flowchart. |
| D29 | Which grant the audit log records | When a clinic has several active grants for one pet, `PetAccessLog.grantId` is the grant that **expires last** (no expiry counts as latest; on a tie, the newest). | `PetAccessLog` holds one `grantId`. The longest-lasting grant is the one that explains why the clinic can still see the pet, which is easiest for an owner to read (US-06). |
| D30 | What counts as authored records | Only **active** health records (`isActive = true`) count as authored for D6, D18 and D20. Soft-deleted records stay in the database for tracing but do not keep the pet visible to the clinic. | A deleted record is shown to no one, so it should not be a reason for the clinic to keep seeing the pet. |
| D31 | Concurrent appointment status changes | Every status change (clinic update, owner cancel, pet archive) is written only if the status is still the one that was checked (compare-and-set inside the transaction). If another change happened first, the request fails with `409` and nothing is written (status, status log, grant). | Without it, an owner cancel and a clinic confirm at the same moment could leave a cancelled appointment with an open grant, breaking D4. |
| D32 | Logging denied views | When a clinic gets `NONE` (404), nothing is written to `PetAccessLog`. Only views that return data are logged (`FULL` with its `grantId`, `AUTHORED_ONLY` with `grantId = null`). | `PetAccessLog` records what a clinic actually saw. A 404 shows nothing. Tracking repeated denied attempts can be a separate table later (backlog). |
| D33 | Access level in clinic responses | Clinic responses for a pet include `accessLevel: 'FULL' \| 'AUTHORED_ONLY'`. | The frontend can choose what to show without guessing from missing fields, and manual testing is easier to read. |
| D34 | Audit log write fails | If writing `PetAccessLog` fails, the whole request fails with `500` and no pet data is returned. | D10 requires every view to be traceable. Returning data without a log would leave a gap in the audit trail. |
| D35 | Writing a record without full access | A clinic with `AUTHORED_ONLY` access gets `403 No active access to this pet`; with `NONE` it gets `404 Pet not found`. | An `AUTHORED_ONLY` clinic already knows the pet exists, so a clear 403 reveals nothing new; `NONE` keeps the D20 404. |
| D36 | Audit log for reading records | Listing a pet's records and reading one record are logged like opening the pet (D24): `grantId` for `FULL`, null for `AUTHORED_ONLY` or own records. Denied reads are not logged (D32). | Health records are the most sensitive data; reading them through the API must leave a trace. |
| D37 | Archived records | A record with `isActive = false` returns 404 to everyone (owner included) on `GET /health-records/:id`, and cannot be edited or get attachments. | D30: a deleted record is shown to no one. |
| D38 | Search fields for `AUTHORED_ONLY` pets | They match on pet name only. Pets with `FULL` access match on all fields, owner contact included. | Searching by phone or email would reveal who owns the pet after access ended (D9). |
| D39 | Logging searches | Searches are not logged. | Search finds a pet; opening it is logged. Logging every result row would flood the log. |
| D40 | Lockout details | A staff account is blocked after 5 failed codes within 15 minutes (D14 gave no window). Attempts made while blocked are not recorded. Codes with the wrong format (not 6 digits) are rejected by validation and not counted. | Without a window, old mistakes would count forever; recording blocked attempts would extend the block without end. |
| D41 | When owner-share time starts | The duration starts when the clinic redeems the code, not when the owner creates it. `durationDays` is required: 7, 30, 90, or `null` for unlimited. | The owner chooses "7 days of access", so it starts when access starts. A required field stops unlimited access being given by mistake. |
| D42 | Redeem error responses | Wrong, expired and used codes all return `400 Invalid or expired code`. A blocked account or clinic gets `429` with the minutes left. | US-03 AC3; the wait time helps staff without revealing anything about codes. |
| D43 | Owner's access list | `GET /pets/:petId/access-grants` shows only active grants, newest first. | Enough to revoke and extend; full history belongs to US-06 (backlog). |
| D44 | Revoke and extend errors | Revoking a clinic with no active access → `404`. Extending an appointment grant or an ended grant → `409` with the reason. | Clear reasons for the frontend; 409 because the request conflicts with the grant's current state. |
| D45 | Several live codes | An owner may have several unused codes at once. | Each code is single use and expires in 15 minutes, so the risk is small and no extra logic is needed. |
| D46 | Database session time zone | Every database connection (app and seed) runs with `TimeZone=UTC`. Data stored before the fix was replaced by reseeding; a backup was kept outside the repo. | `@prisma/adapter-pg` sends timestamps without a UTC offset, so a server set to Asia/Bangkok stored them 7 hours early. Setting it in code works on any machine, with no server configuration. |
| D47 | Test database | Integration tests use a separate database, `petwell_test`, configured in `backend/.env.test` (git-ignored). `test/setup-env.ts` stops the run if `DATABASE_URL` is not `petwell_test`. | Tests empty the database on every run. A separate database keeps dev data and manual test accounts safe, and makes runs repeatable. |
| D48 | Test level | Automated access tests call the services directly against `petwell_test` and check the thrown error. HTTP-level checks (guards, roles, status codes end to end) stay in the Python scripts in `scripts/`. | The 404/403/409 rules are thrown inside the services, so this tests them directly without extra packages (`@nestjs/testing`, `supertest`) or JWT setup. The scripts already cover the HTTP layer. |
| D49 | When to merge V2 | Keep `feat/consent-access` unmerged until the remaining frontend (owner share-code screens, access list, revoke, staff `AUTHORED_ONLY` view) is done. The 7-day timebox is extended for this. | The backend and tests are complete, but without the owner screens the main V2 flow cannot be demoed end to end. |
| D50 | Frontend extension after the timebox | The extension for the remaining frontend (D49) is capped at **2 working days**. If it is not done by then, cut in this order: the extend button, then the access-level badge in search; then merge. | Keeps the extension bounded. The cut order follows the timebox rule: frontend first, least important first. |
| D51 | Owner share-code screen | A **Clinic access** card on the owner's pet page (the access list from D52 is in the same card). Default duration is **7 days**. The code is shown once with an mm:ss countdown; at 0 it is hidden and a **Generate new code** button appears. | The shortest duration is the safest default. A live countdown stops owners reading out an expired code. One card keeps share, list and revoke together without a new page. |
| D52 | Owner access list | One row per clinic (revoke works per clinic, D12), listing each active grant's kind and end date. Owner-share grants have a **Change** action (7 / 30 / 90 days or unlimited, from now, D27); appointment grants do not. Revoke uses a confirmation inside the row (no browser dialog) that says the appointment is not cancelled. The list refreshes after a change; a code redeemed while the page is open shows after a reload. | Matches how revoke and extend work in the backend, so the owner is not surprised that both kinds of access end. A confirmation in the row avoids blocking browser dialogs. Live updates do not fit the timebox. |
| D53 | Staff pet page by access level | `AUTHORED_ONLY`: name and species only, a limited-access notice that says how to get access again (share code or new appointment), and the clinic's own records; no owner contact and no add-record form. `FULL`: the whole pet, owner contact (D9) and the add-record form. A `403` when adding a record (access ended while the page was open, D35) shows a message and reloads into the limited view. | Staff should see why data is missing and what to do next, not an error. Hiding the form avoids a 403 in the normal case; the message covers access that ends while the page is open. |
| D54 | Access level in staff search | Only `AUTHORED_ONLY` results get a badge (**Own records only**); `FULL` results have none. The empty state says these pets are found by pet name only (D38). | `FULL` is the normal case, so labelling only the exception keeps the list quiet and explains why contact details are missing. |

## 3. Findings from code review (2026-11-09)

- **Grants must be stored records**, created at the first CONFIRMED and ended by REJECTED / CANCELLED / COMPLETED + 30 days. They must not be derived from the current status. Reason: `RESCHEDULED` can follow `CONFIRMED` (clinic had access) or `PENDING` (never confirmed), and the status alone cannot tell them apart.
- **Three code paths change appointment status**: clinic status update, owner cancel, and pet archive (cancels live appointments). Access rules must be applied in all three, preferably through one shared function.
- **Clinics can cancel appointments too**, not only owners.
- The appointment list already exposes only `pet { id, name, species }` to the clinic, so no extra change is needed before access is granted.
- `Pet.ownerId` is currently **required**. D8 (pets created by a clinic) needs it to become optional.
- `HealthRecord.clinicId` already identifies the authoring clinic, which makes D6 a simple filter.
- See `PetWell_Impact_Analysis.md` for the full list of affected endpoints.

## 4. Findings during implementation (2026-11-13)

- `GET /health-records/:recordId` let **any clinic read any record by id**, including archived ones. It was not in the impact analysis; closed with D36 and D37.
- **Timestamps written by the app were stored 7 hours early** (the UTC time was saved as if it were Bangkok time). The app read them back the same way, so access checks still worked, but Prisma Studio and SQL using `now()` showed shifted times. Pre-existing since v1. Cause confirmed with a read-only probe and fixed by D46.
- The frontend must handle `AUTHORED_ONLY` responses, which have no `owner`: the staff pet search page reads `pet.owner` directly.
- (2026-11-20) The staff pet page did not show owner contact even with `FULL` access, although D9 allows it. Added with D53.

## 5. Out of scope / backlog

- Pets with no owner in the system, created by a clinic (D8, US-05 — deferred by D21)
- Owner claims a pet that a clinic created (D8 follow-up)
- Clinic requests access and the owner approves
- Owner selects a clinic and shares directly, without a code (D15 follow-up)
- Clinic-private notes not shown to the owner (considered for D19, not adopted)
- Owner chooses the scope of what is shared (all history vs. selected records)
- Frozen snapshot of history at the date access ended
- Owner-facing view of the audit log ("who viewed my pet")
- Review of what owner contact info a clinic may keep after access ends (D9 follow-up)
- Backfill grants for appointments confirmed before V2 (D28 follow-up)
- Owner-facing history of ended grants (D43 follow-up)
- Logging denied attempts to open a pet, e.g. to spot a clinic probing ids (D32 follow-up)
- Health record edit history: who changed what and when, with previous values kept (today only `createdById` and `updatedAt` are stored)
- Vaccine name on health records, so the dashboard can show overdue vaccines that no later record covers (D70 follow-up)
- Email vaccine reminders (SRS stretch goal); clinic search by area; owner cancel only 72 hours or more before the visit (SRS 7.5) — not built
